Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Expense Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. Our primary architecture is built on a privacy-first, offline-first approach: we do not maintain external servers to collect or store your personal financial data.

1. Offline-First Local Data Storage

All expense entries, account details, transaction categories, and custom tags you input are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage.

  • No transaction data is transmitted to, processed by, or stored on our servers.
  • If you clear the App's storage cache or uninstall the App without a backup, your local data will be permanently removed.

2. Optional Google Drive Cloud Backup (Consent-Based)

The App allows you to create backups and synchronize data using your personal Google Drive account.

  • Explicit User Consent: Google Drive integration is disabled by default and activates only when you explicitly connect your Google account.
  • Restricted Scope: The App uses the minimum necessary Google Drive permissions (such as drive.appdata or drive.file) to store and retrieve only the backup files created specifically by [App Name].
  • Access Restrictions: We have no visibility into your Google credentials, nor do we access, view, or alter any other files stored inside your Google Drive.
  • Revocation: You can disconnect your Google account and stop cloud backups at any time directly through the App settings or your Google Account security dashboard.

3. Payment App Notification Detection

To help you track expenses automatically, the App offers an optional notification listener feature to parse transaction alerts.

  • User-Selected Apps Only: The App only monitors notifications from payment and banking applications you explicitly choose and enable in the App settings.
